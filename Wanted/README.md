# Wanted: historical constructions, in progress

The charts list known historical approximations that are not charted yet, under "Wanted" on each
chart. A construction is charted under a historical name only once it has been checked against the
original source, step by step, so that the credit is for what that person actually drew.

Drafts here are constructions of the historical value, made to be checked against the source and
corrected where they differ, then charted (with `tools/verify.py`) under their discoverer's name.

To check against de Gelder's own figure before charting. |
