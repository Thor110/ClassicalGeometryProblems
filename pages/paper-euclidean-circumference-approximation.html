Method for Approximating the Circumference of Any Given Circle

Step through it live on Constructions → · Chart: Rectifying The Circle →

Here is a method I devised to approximate the circumference of any given circle, strictly adhering to the Euclidean rule set. (straightedge and compass only)

Above is a diagram of the construction and below are the steps for reproducing it.

  1. Let O=(0,0). Construct the unit circle centred at O. Let B=(1,0) and C=(−1,0) be the right/left cardinal points.
  2. Connect points BC and draw a perpendicular bisector using BC creating points D=(0,1) and E=(0,−1).
  3. At point B draw the line perpendicular to DE (this is the vertical line x=1 through B). At point C draw the line perpendicular to DE (the vertical line x=−1 through C).
  4. At points D and E draw the lines perpendicular to BC (these are the horizontal lines y=1 and y=−1). Extend the line through E=(0,−1) far to the right (this extended horizontal line will act as the baseline for the final intersection). These four lines complete the circumscribing set of four squares about the circle.
  5. Draw the line DB joining D=(0,1) (top-middle) to B=(1,0) (right-middle).
  6. Draw the ray OH where H=(1,1) is the top-right corner of the circumscribing square. Let J=OH∩(circle) (the point where the ray meets the circle) and let L=OH∩DB.
  7. Let M be the midpoint of segment LJ. Construct the perpendicular bisector of segment MJ.
  8. Let N be the right-side intersection point of that perpendicular bisector at MJ.
  9. Let F be the top-left corner of the circumscribing arrangement: F=(−1,1). Draw the line FN.
  10. Let K be the intersection of line FN with the extended baseline (the horizontal line through E, i.e. G=(−1,−1), extended far to the right).
  11. The constructed length L is the horizontal segment from the left-baseline anchor G=(−1,−1) to the point K. Its numeric value for the unit circle is the coefficient α (so for a circle of radius r the constructed length equals α⋅r).

Algebraic Formula

Algebraic formula, instructions and detailed comparison to 2π (derived by GPT-5).

α = 2 · (1 + b + s₀) ⁄ (1 − b + s₀)
b = 1⁄8 + 3 ⁄ (4√2),   s₀ = ½ √(2 − 4b²)
  • α ≈ 6.295357530626529 (unit circle coefficient)
  • 2π ≈ 6.283185307179586
  • Absolute gap: α − 2π ≈ 0.012172223446943
  • Percentage of accuracy: (GK / Ȼ) × 100 = 99.806647622550144561036880384858% (Ȼ denotes the actual circumference of the circle.)
