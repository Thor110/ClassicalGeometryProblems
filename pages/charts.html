Method Names
Names such as Circling the Square, Array Casting and Cube 2 Tesseract are the names Edward James Gordon gave his own families of methods, and the chapter titles of his paper. They are not standard terms, and not the names of the problems themselves:
    Reading The Chart
    • Base ruleset: at most 25 circles and 40 lines.
    • Extended ruleset: at most 49 circles and 80 lines, for constructions at 99.91% and above.
    • Accepted range: 95% minimum, 100% maximum.
    • Counted: circles and lines. Not counted: macro scale midpoints, those easy to reproduce on paper.
    • ~ Contested: the original discoverer is being sought.
    • @ Too simple: likely found many times before.
    • µ Micro scale edits: some steps would be hard to reproduce on paper.
    • Verified reproduced by someone other than its method holder, a person or an AI checker. Self-verified checked only by its method holder. Disputed an open dispute. The rules.
    A contested or too-simple mark is not a judgement of the person. It records that a construction may have been found before, so credit can go to whoever found it first. Full ruleset.
