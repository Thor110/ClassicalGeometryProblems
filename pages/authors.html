The Authors
Most of the people here are remembered for these constructions alone, and some are known only from a single paper or a review of it; their cards say so rather than guess. Others are among the great names of mathematics and science, and tried their hand at the same problems. Cards are in order of each author's earliest entry. Each lists the entries credited to them, with links to their place on the charts, and to fuller biographies where there are any.
