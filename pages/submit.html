Classical Geometry Problems // Submit An Approximation

Found a construction? Measure it, write it up, and add it to the charts. Every honest attempt is welcome.
How To Submit
  1. Draw your construction in GeoGebra using only circles and lines: straightedge and compass, no measuring.
  2. Measure the constructed value and the true value with GeoGebra's tools, and work out the accuracy with the calculator below.
  3. Fill in the form below. It writes your submission in the archive's template.
  4. Open the submission form with the button below (it fills in what it can), or send a pull request adding your .ggb and .md files beside the others. Include a public GeoGebra link so anyone can check it.
  5. Your entry is then verified under the verification rules, by people and by the automated check, and its record is shown on the chart.
Not sure it's good enough? Submit it anyway. A first attempt at 95% teaches as much as a record, and every attempt has a place on the charts.

The Ruleset
  • Base ruleset: at most 25 circles and 40 lines.
  • Extended ruleset: at most 49 circles and 80 lines, for constructions at 99.91% and above.
  • Range: 95% minimum, 100% maximum.
  • Counted: only the circles and lines the result depends on. Not counted: exploration, intersections, macro scale midpoints, the given figure, and the result itself. See the counting rules.
  • Key marks: ~ contested, @ too simple, µ micro scale edits.
If your GeoGebra file gives a more precise readout than three decimal places, include it as well. Full ruleset.
