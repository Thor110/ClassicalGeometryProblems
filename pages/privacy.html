Classical Geometry Problems // Extension Privacy

What the submission extension reads, what it keeps, and where it sends it.
What It Reads
  • Only the GeoGebra tab you are on, and only when you click the extension. It reads the construction (the GeoGebra file and the list of its objects and measurements) so it can preview the accuracy and send the file.
  • It reads nothing from any other tab or site, and nothing runs in the background.
What It Keeps
  • Your name, so you don't have to type it again.
  • If you sign in, your GitHub sign-in token and GitHub username. These stay in your browser's extension storage on your computer. Sign out in the extension deletes them, and you can also revoke the extension's access at any time in GitHub's settings under Applications.
  • Nothing else is stored, and there is no analytics or tracking of any kind.
What It Sends, And Where
  • To GitHub, only when you click Submit: your construction, your name, and the details you filled in, as a pull request to the public archive at github.com/Thor110/ClassicalGeometryProblems. To do this it makes a copy (fork) of the archive under your GitHub account. The sign-in asks for the public_repo permission, which is what GitHub requires for creating a fork and opening a pull request. The extension uses it for nothing else.
  • Download As Zip saves the files to your computer and sends nothing.
  • Open As An Issue opens GitHub's issue form in a new tab, filled in with your details, for you to check and send yourself.
  • Nothing is sent to any other server. The archive has no server of its own.
A submission is public and permanent: that is the point of the archive. Your name is published with your construction, so that the discovery stays credited to you.
Questions
The extension's code is open, in the extension folder of the archive. Questions can be asked by opening an issue.
