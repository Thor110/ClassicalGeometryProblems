The Three Classic Geometry Problems
Edward James Gordon's account of searching for approximations to all three problems, from first pencil drawings to GeoGebra: Circling the Square, Cube 2 Tesseract and Array Casting, with 46 figures. It opens with why he tried in the first place.
35 pages · Squaring the Circle · Trisecting an Angle · Doubling the Cube · PDF
Euclidean Circumference Approximation
A one-circle construction of a straight line 99.806% as long as a circle's circumference, with exact coordinates for every step and a closed formula for the result.
1 page · Rectifying the Circle · Step through it live · PDF
Rope Around The Clock
A mechanical method: a rope and peg jig that divides any length into exact thirds, and with it trisects any angle. Also asks whether it rediscovers the ancient mesolabe.
7 pages · Mechanical solution · See it animated · PDF
