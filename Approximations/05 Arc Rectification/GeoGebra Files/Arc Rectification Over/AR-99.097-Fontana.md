**Description:**
Beutel: "Um den Kreisbogen OA (Mittelpunkt M) zu rektifizieren, errichtet man in O auf OM das Lot OL und zieht OA. Halbiert man den Winkel AOL durch OC, COL durch OD usw. und ist AC ⟂ OA, CD ⟂ OC, DE ⟂ OD usf., so erhält man schon nach wenigen Halbierungen einen Punkt Z als Schnittpunkt des zuletzt errichteten Lots mit OL", which with careful drawing coincides with the true length.

In the file, Beutel's O is B and his M is O: the tangent at B; the chord BA and the perpendicular to it at A; the bisector of the angle between the chord and the tangent meets that perpendicular at C; the perpendicular to BC at C and the next bisector give D; then E; the perpendicular at E meets the tangent at Z.

**Notes / Limitations:**
A convergent construction: with k bisections BZ = 2^(k+1) tan(x/2^(k+1)), a quarter as far off with each bisection, against a thirty-second for Cusa's point worked on the halved arc (see Variants/). With three, as drawn, it is 0.9% long at 150°; Beutel's "a few bisections" are enough for a drawing of small arcs. Beutel gives no first name; it may be Gregorio Fontana.
