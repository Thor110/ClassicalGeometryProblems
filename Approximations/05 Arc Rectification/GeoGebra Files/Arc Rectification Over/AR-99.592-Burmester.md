**Description:**
The Jahrbuch: "Es sei der Bogen as < 90° und man ergänze ihn zu einem Halbkreis asb mit dem Mittelpunkt m. Um den Mittelpunkt 𝔪 von bm beschreibe man einen Kreis vom Radius 𝔪a, der von 𝔪s in 𝔰 getroffen wird. Die Sehne a𝔰 stellt die fragliche Strecke dar."

In the file Burmester's a is B, his s is A, and his 𝔪 and 𝔰 are m and S: B′ is the other end of the diameter through B, m the middle of B′O, and the circle about m through B meets mA produced at S. BS is the arc.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Charted at 90°, the end of Burmester's range. Two constructions, d'Ocagne's (1907) and this one, of one and the same length: they share the chart figure.
