**Description:**
Van den Berg: "Uit deze beide waarden vindt men nog, door 4/5 de van de tweede op te tellen bij 1/5 de van de eerste, eene meer benaderde waarde."

In the file: Rankine's first gives D on the tangent at B; his second gives C₂, and the circle about C₂ through A carries CA on along the tangent to X, so BX = BC + CA. Y is a fifth of the way from X to D (five radii along the perpendicular at X, the line from the fifth to D and the parallel through the first), so BY = (4 BX + BD)/5.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Its error is of the seventh order, θ⁷/870912, against the fifth order of each construction alone. At 150° the fifth of the gap from X to D is about 3.5% of the radius, but the step the checker measures stays above 5%.
