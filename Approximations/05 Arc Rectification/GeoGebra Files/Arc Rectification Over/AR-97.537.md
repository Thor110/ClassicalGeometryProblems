**Description:**
The arc runs from D round to the given angle. Tangents to the circle at D, at the point a quarter turn round (F) and at the arc's end meet at G and C; the path D–G–C is the constructed length. For an arc of θ it measures 2r + r·tan((θ − 90°)/2), against the true rθ.

**Notes / Limitations:**
95% or better for arcs from 128° to 215°, measured by sweeping the angle. Below about 128° it falls away quickly, and at 90° the construction breaks down (the tangents at F and at the arc's end coincide).
