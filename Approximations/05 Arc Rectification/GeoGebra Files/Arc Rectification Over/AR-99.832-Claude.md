**Description:**
Lambert's rectification, a quarter versine (see the entry ar-over-99.831), worked on the arc halved once and then multiplied back:

1. Bisect the angle once from B: H₁, so the arc BH is 1/2 of the arc BA.
2. On the arc BH: P a radius beyond E, moved towards O by a quarter of the versine of BH (two midpoints) to P'; P'H meets the tangent at B in D. BD is very nearly the arc BH.
3. Double the length once along the tangent: the circle about D through B meets the tangent again at twice the distance; repeat from that point. The last, 2 times BD, is the constructed length.

**Notes / Limitations:**
