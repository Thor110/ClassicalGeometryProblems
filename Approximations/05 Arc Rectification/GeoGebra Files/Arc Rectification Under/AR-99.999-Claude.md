**Description:**
Cusa's rectification (see the entry ar-under-95.492), worked on the arc halved five times and then multiplied back:

1. Bisect the angle five times from B: H₁ to H_5, so the arc BH is 1/32 of the arc BA.
2. On the arc BH: P a radius beyond E, the far end of the diameter through B; PH meets the tangent at B in D. BD is very nearly the arc BH.
3. Double the length five times along the tangent: the circle about D through B meets the tangent again at twice the distance; repeat from that point. The last, 32 times BD, is the constructed length.

**Notes / Limitations:**
A general method, charted, like Dürer's and the search finds, at its weakest angle from 30° to 150°. Its smallest step is 6.2% of the radius, above the 5% of the micro scale rule. Credit for the construction is Nicholas of Cusa and Snellius's; only the halving is new.
