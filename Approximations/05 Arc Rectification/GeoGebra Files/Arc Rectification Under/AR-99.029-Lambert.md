**Description:**
Vahlen: "Man verschiebe den Snelliusschen Rektifikationspunkt um [1/5] sin vers x nach dem Mittelpunkt des Kreises hin" - move Snellius' rectification point towards the centre by a fifth of the versine. Snellius' point P is the one in Cusa's construction, a radius beyond the far end E of the diameter; the versine of the arc BA is BC, where C is the foot of the perpendicular from A to OB.

In the file: the tangent at B; the perpendicular from A gives C; five steps of BC up the tangent and a parallel give BY = BC/5; P' is that far from P towards O. The line P'A meets the tangent at D, and BD stands for the arc.

**Notes / Limitations:**
With P' at (9 + cos x)/5 radii beyond O, BD = sin x (14 + cos x)/(9 + 6 cos x) of the radius, the formula Newton gave in his letter to Leibniz of 13 June 1676 and Lambert proved; the scan garbles the fraction, but only 1/5 gives that formula. Much better than Cusa's for larger arcs (99.03% at 90°, against 95.49%). At 90° C falls on O.
