**Description:**
Van den Berg: "Maak AC = ½BA, beschrijf uit C den cirkelboog BD, dan snijdt deze ... de raaklijn af AD = θ − θ⁵/1080 ..." - extend the chord by half of itself, draw the arc about the new point through the far end, and it cuts off the arc's length on the tangent.

In the file the tangent point is B and the far end A (van den Berg's letters swapped): the chord AB, its midpoint M, the circle about B through M gives C beyond B; the circle about C through A meets the tangent at B in D.

**Notes / Limitations:**
A general method for arcs of any size; very good for small arcs (θ⁵/1080), falling to 95.2% at 150°. The scan garbles the fraction; only a half gives van den Berg's series.
