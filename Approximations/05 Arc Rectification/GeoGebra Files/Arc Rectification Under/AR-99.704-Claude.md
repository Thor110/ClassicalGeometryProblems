**Description:**
Lambert's rectification (see the entry ar-under-99.029), worked on the arc halved once and then multiplied back:

1. Bisect the angle once from B: H₁, so the arc BH is 1/2 of the arc BA.
2. On the arc BH: P a radius beyond E, moved towards O by a fifth of the versine of BH (five steps of it up the tangent and a parallel) to P'; P'H meets the tangent at B in D. BD is very nearly the arc BH.
3. Double the length once along the tangent: the circle about D through B meets the tangent again at twice the distance; repeat from that point. The last, 2 times BD, is the constructed length.

**Notes / Limitations:**
A general method, charted, like Dürer's and the search finds, at its weakest angle from 30° to 150°. Its smallest step is 11.4% of the radius, above the 5% of the micro scale rule. Credit for the construction is Lambert's; only the halving is new.
