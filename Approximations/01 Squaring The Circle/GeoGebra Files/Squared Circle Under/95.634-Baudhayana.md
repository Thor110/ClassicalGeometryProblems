**Description:**
Thibaut's translation: "Or else divide (the diameter) into fifteen parts and remove two; that (the remaining thirteen parts) is the gross side of the square."

In the file AB is the diameter. Half a radius is stepped out fifteen times on the perpendicular at A, and the parallel through the thirteenth step to the line from the fifteenth to B meets AB at X. The square is raised on AX.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Under by 4.4% in area, just inside the charts' 95% floor. The Sutras call it the "gross" side. Common to all three Sulba Sutras; the entry is under Baudhāyana, the oldest.
