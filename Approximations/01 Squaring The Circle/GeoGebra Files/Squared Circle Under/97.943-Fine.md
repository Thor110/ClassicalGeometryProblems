**Description:**
Finé (in Buteo's quotation): "Sit descriptus circa centrum A circulus BCD, cuius dimetiens BD, intra quem describatur quadratum EF ... eidem circulo BCD circunscribatur quadratum BGD. Post modum ab angulo E ipsius inscripti quadrati ad circunscripti angulum G recta linea ducatur, quae secet diametrum BD in puncto H, circulum vero BCD in puncto I. Deinde ex data linea recta quae sit ipsius AH dupla per datum punctum H quadratum rursum describatur HLM ... Consequenter a puncto I ad punctum L recta ducatur IL, quae secet eundem diametrum BD in puncto N. Et centro A, intervallo autem AN circulus describatur NO ... Huic demum NO circunscribatur quadratum NOP." Finé claims NOP equals the circle.

In the file Finé's A is O and his diameter BD is the y axis (B = Bt). E is found on the bisector of the right angle, G where the tangents at A and Bt meet; L on the same bisector at the height of H. The square is drawn about the circle of radius ON.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Under by 2.1%. Finé's second squaring (1544) needs two mean proportionals, which he finds with a mechanical gnomon; it cannot be drawn with straightedge and compass.
