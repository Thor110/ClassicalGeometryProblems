**Description:**
Scaliger: "Sit diametrus AC circuli propositi ABCD expositarum partium XX. Ex interminatis QR, NM sese normaliter secantibus in N abscindantur NO quidem ipsi EA, NR autem XVIII vicesimis diametri AC aequalis ... Ex NM et QR abscindantur NH, NQ ipsi BD aequales ... Et propterea circa QR semicirculo descripto QMR, recta NM media proportionalis inter NR, NQ, id est, NH, vel EA, erit aequalis τῷ ἐμβαδῷ prius invento." His BD is the side of the inscribed equilateral triangle. Corollary: "circuli aream esse aequalem rectangulo sub latere trianguli aequilateri in eo ipso inscripti circulo, et novem decimis diametri concepto."

In the file N is the end A of the diameter. NQ is laid off to the left with the circle about A whose radius is the triangle's side P₁P₂; NR, 9/10 of the diameter, reaches to four fifths of the radius beyond O (five radii up the y axis and a parallel). The semicircle on QR meets the perpendicular at A in M, and the square is drawn on AM.
