**Description:**
In Hayden's words: "In and about a given circle, as AfEe, draw the inscribed and tangent squares in the manner indicated by the figure, with their diagonals. Draw the diameter AE bisecting the opposite sides of the said squares; bisect AB in C, and DE in F; set off from E and D respectively EG and DI equal to DF, EF and join GI; from E let fall EH perpendicular to GI, and with radius EH describe the circle HK; draw the line CL touching the circle HK, and cutting the circle AfEe in P; then through the point P, parallel to AB, draw MN terminating in its intersections with the diagonals, which will be one side of a square very nearly equal to the circle."

In the file: A is the top of the circle and E the bottom; B is the corner of the outer square beside A, so C is halfway along its top side; D is where the inner square's lower side crosses AE; G is on the tangent at E and I on AE beyond D. The tangent from C to the circle HK is drawn with the circle on CE as diameter, and the square on MN is completed below it.
