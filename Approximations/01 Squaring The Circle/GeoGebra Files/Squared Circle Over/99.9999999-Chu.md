**Description:**
Chu's left figure (here on the diameter of the given circle, with his A the centre and AB = AC = 1, the radius):
(i) AD = AB/5 (five radii up the perpendicular, and a parallel). (ii) The circle about D through B meets BC at E; F on it with BF = AD. (iii) The semicircle on DB; G on it with BG = EA. (iv) HB perpendicular to BC, HB = 1. (v) CI = 3CH/5 (five radii along CB from C, and a parallel); IK = 1 on CH extended. (vi) The semicircle on CK; L on it with IL perpendicular to CK, and M with I the midpoint of LM. (vii) N on IL with NI = DG. (viii) The semicircle on LM; O on it with NO perpendicular to LM.
Then EF = √63/5 and NO = √(15√5 − 7)/5.

His right figure (on the perpendicular diameter, with Q the centre and R on the circle): RQ = 1, PR = 1/4 (two midpoints), SQ = 3/2 perpendicular to RQ; T the midpoint of SP; UT = SR, perpendicular to PS (U is labelled V in the file). Then PU = √269/8.

Putting them together (Chu leaves this to "basic constructions"): y = (5/8)·EF·NO/PU, by a fourth proportional on two diameters and three midpoints; z = √(y · 5y/2), the mean proportional of y and 5y/2; the side of the square is the hypotenuse on the legs EF and z, so its square is EF² (1 + (5/2)(5NO/8PU)²) = (63/25)(1 + (5/2)(15√5 − 7)/269).

**Notes / Limitations:**
