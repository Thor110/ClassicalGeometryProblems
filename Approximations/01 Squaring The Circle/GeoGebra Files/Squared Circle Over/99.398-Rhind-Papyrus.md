**Description:**
Problem 50 of the papyrus finds the area of a round field 9 khet across by taking away a ninth of the diameter and squaring the remaining 8: 64 setat. So the square on 8/9 of the diameter stands for the circle, π ≈ 4 · (8/9)² = 256/81 = 3.1605.

The drawing: the diameter AB; nine equal steps up the perpendicular at A, by doubling (one radius, then 2, 4 and 8 steps, then one more); the line from the ninth step to B and the parallel through the eighth cut AB at X, so AX = 8/9 AB. The square on AX.

**Notes / Limitations:**
The papyrus states the rule as arithmetic, with no figure, so only the 8/9 is the scribe's; how the ninths are drawn here is the archive's choice. It is far from the best on the chart, but it is the oldest known approximation to the circle's area.
