**Description:**
Anglin: "Let ABCD be the circumscribed square, and EF a side of inscribed square of the given circle, AC and DKB diagonals of the former. Draw a tangent to the circle at K meeting AB at G (or mark off AG equal to EF), and join GC cutting the diagonal DB in H. Then HF is very nearly equal to the side of a square equal in area to the given circle."

In the file: the square about the circle from its four tangents; K and F where the diagonal DB meets the circle, K nearer B; the tangent at K meets AB at G; GC meets DB at H; the square is raised on HF.
