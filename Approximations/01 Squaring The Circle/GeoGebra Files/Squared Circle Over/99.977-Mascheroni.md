**Description:**
Mascheroni: "Trouver le côté d'un quarré qui approche le plus d'être égal en surface à un cercle d'un rayon donné AB. Solution. Faites sur la circonférence BPCQDRE, AB = BC = CD = DE; du même rayon BA et des centres B et E, décrivez les deux arcs ALc, AMd; des centres C et D et du rayon DB, décrivez les arcs cNM, dNL; faites AN = BP = PQ; puis LM = QR, BR sera le côté cherché."

In the file A is the centre. C, D and E are stepped round the circle from B with the radius. The circles about B and E through A, and the circles about C and D with the radius DB, give N (where the last two meet), L (about D and about B) and M (about C and about E). The chord AN is laid twice round the circle from B, to P and Q, and the chord LM once more, to R. The square is raised on BR.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Long by 0.0002 of the radius, 0.023% by area. Every step is a compass step; the square itself is drawn with lines only to show the result. Mascheroni gives it as the practical choice over constructions that are closer in theory but longer.
