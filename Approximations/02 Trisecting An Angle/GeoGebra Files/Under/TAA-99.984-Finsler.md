**Description:**
"Auf einer Geraden werde mit beliebiger Einheit OA = m, ŌA = n und MA = 3mn/(2(m + n)) abgetragen. Ist dann ∢BOA mit MB = MA der durch k-maliges Halbieren gefundene Winkel φ/m, so ist der Winkel BŌA = ψ sehr nahe gleich φ/n."

In the file Finsler's A is B and the unit is a quarter of the radius. Ō is Ob, a quarter of OB from O. MA = 18/7 units is drawn as 1½ times 12/7, the crossing height of the lines joining the tops of perpendiculars 4 and 3 units long at O and B (the archive's way to the length). The angle is halved twice, the circle about M through B meets the second bisector at Bb, and the angle at Ō is carried to O by a parallel.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Under by 16.7" at 90° (0.015%). Its error is about 4½ times smaller than that of (8.3), and with the same 45° steps it stays under 0.02" for any angle.
