**Description:**
Ludolph (in Dutch): the half circle BDC and the diameter BC are each divided into four equal parts; from the points F and G of the half circle lines are drawn to the centre A, and from the points H and O of the diameter perpendiculars, which meet them at M and N. The triangle BNC is drawn, and the circle BICL about it; it cuts the half diameter at I. "Desen grootsten deel [IA] is minder (oft corter) als den Diameter, wiens omloop gelijck is den Diameter BC."

In the file Ludolph's A is O and his B and C are A and C; his D is Bt. F is found by bisecting the right angle, O by halving OC. The centre of the circle through A, N and C is found on the y axis, and I where it crosses OBt. Since OI stands for the diameter of a circle as long as AC, the circumference of the given circle is AC · AC / OI, laid off along OC by a parallel, to X.
