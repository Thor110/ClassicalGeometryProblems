**Description:**
355/113 = 3 + 4²/(7² + 8²). In the circle about C through A, with CD the radius at right angles to CA:

1. Lay off CE = 7/8 of CD (by halving three times), and join E to A.
2. On AE, from A, lay off AF = 1/2.
3. Draw FG parallel to CD, meeting CA at G, and join E to G.
4. Draw FH parallel to EG, meeting CA at H. Then AH = 4²/(7² + 8²) = 16/113 of the radius.
5. Three radii laid off beyond A, to P₃, complete HP₃ = 355/113 of the radius: half the circumference, very nearly.

Steps 1 to 4 are de Gelder's, as Hobson gives them. De Gelder needed only the length AH; step 5, which lays out the half circumference as a single length, is the archive's, so that it can be measured like every other rectification.

**Notes / Limitations:**
355/113 is the value known in China since the fifth century as Milü (Zu Chongzhi). Ramanujan's 1913 squaring of the circle on the squaring chart is built on the same value.
