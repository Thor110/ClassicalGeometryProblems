**Description:**
Scaliger: "Ex recta HD abscindatur recta HE aequalis ipsi HC. Connectatur recta AE. Recta igitur AH erit longitudine tripla rectae HE, potentia autem nonupla ... Quadratum autem AE aequale est quadratis AH, HE, per XLVII primi. Erit ergo decuplum quadrati HE ... Ergo perimetrus erit quadrupla ipsius AE. Quare si sumatur longitudo FG quadrupla longitudinis AE, habebis rectam aequalem perimetro circuli ABCD."

In the file his diameter AC is vertical, from Bt down to D. H is the middle of OD (where the chord through the points a sixth of the circle from D meets the y axis), and E is on that chord with HE = HD. Four times BtE is the circumference.
