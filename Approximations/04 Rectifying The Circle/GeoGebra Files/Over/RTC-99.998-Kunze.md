**Description:**
Kunze (1842): "Man theilt den Durchmesser in fünf gleiche Theile und macht ein rechtwinkeliges Dreieck ABC, dessen Katheten AB = 6 und BC = 3 solcher Theile halten. Dieses Dreieck hat mit dem Kreise sehr nahe einerlei Umfang."

Vahlen: "Ein rechtwinkliges Dreieck, dessen Katheten 6/5 und 3/5 des Durchmessers sind, hat den Umfang 1,8 + (3/5)√5 = 3,1416407 (in Teilen des Durchmessers)" - a right triangle whose legs are 6/5 and 3/5 of the diameter has a perimeter of 3.1416407 diameters.

In the file: a fifth of the radius, OX, by five radii up the perpendicular at O and a parallel; OL₁ = 6/5 of the radius (3/5 of the diameter) along OA, and OL₃ = 12/5 of the radius (6/5 of the diameter) down the perpendicular; the hypotenuse L₁L₃. The perimeter is then laid out on the line OA: from L₁ back through O, on by OL₃ to M₁, and on by L₁L₃ to M₂. L₁M₂ is the constructed circumference.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Kunze's perimeter is 6/5 · (1 + φ) diameters, where φ is the golden ratio: the same number that Kunze's own square on GH (1842, later given by Hobson) gives as an area and Dixon found again in 1991, so the two entries share their precise readout. Kunze gives the triangle; how its sides are drawn and laid end to end is the archive's.
