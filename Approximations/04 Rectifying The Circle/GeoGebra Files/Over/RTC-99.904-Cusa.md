**Description:**
Buteo: "Esto circulus ABCD in quo centrum E. Et agantur angulis sese rectis decussantes diametri BD et CA. Et intra circulum statuatur linea recta AF, secans diametron BD in signo G, ita ut GE sit ipsius FA dimidium, quod sit KA, et connectantur KE. Ait Cusanus lineam FA esse aequalem peripheriae BA. Quod nequaquam verum est, sed maior est FA ipsa peripheria BA."

In the file Cusa's E is O, his A is Bt and his BD is the x axis. With M the middle of OBt, CZ = CM − MO = r/φ; a semicircle on W C (W at r/φ to the left of O) gives OY, the mean proportional of r and r/φ, which is carried to the x axis at G. The chord from Bt through G meets the circle at F; four times BtF is the circumference.
