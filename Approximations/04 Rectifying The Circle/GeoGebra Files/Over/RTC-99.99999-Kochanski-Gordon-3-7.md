**Description:**
Starts from the Kochanski Gordon Crossover file (RTC-99.9996-Kochanski-Gordon.ggb), which lays Kochański's length (to S) and Edward's (to P) along one line from K and takes their midpoint. Here, instead, a perpendicular is drawn at S, seven equal compass steps of length SP are marked up it, the seventh mark is joined to P, and a parallel through the third mark meets the line at Z, three sevenths of the way from S to P. KZ is the constructed circumference.
