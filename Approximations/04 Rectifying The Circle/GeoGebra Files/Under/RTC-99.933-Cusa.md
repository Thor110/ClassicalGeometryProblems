**Description:**
Buteo (after Regiomontanus): "Sit circulus ABCD super centro E descriptus, quem duae diametri suae AC et BD quadrent, educaturque altera earum BD utrinque ad longitudinem indefinitam. Latus trianguli aequilateri inscriptibilis huic circulo sit AF, cui ponatur aequalis AG, super G itaque facto centro secundum distantiam GA, circulus describatur, cuius circumferentia secet diametrum BD, ut supra utrinque prolongatam in punctis H et K. Dicitur lineam rectam HK aequalem esse semicircumferentiae BAD."

In the file Cusa's E is O, his A is A and his diameter BD is the y axis. F is a third of the circle from A (the circle about C through O), G is laid off from A along the x axis, and the circle about G through A meets the y axis at H and K.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Under by 0.067%. Regiomontanus found it "much smaller" than the half circumference; it is in fact close.
