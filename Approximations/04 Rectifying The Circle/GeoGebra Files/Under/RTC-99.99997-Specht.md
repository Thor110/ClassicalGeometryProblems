**Description:**
In Hobson's words: "At A make AB = (2 + 1/5) radius on the tangent at A and let BC = 2/5 radius. On the diameter through A take AD = OB, and draw DE parallel to OC."

Then AE/AD = AC/AO, so AE = (13/5) · (√146 / 5) = (13/25) √146 of the radius, very nearly the whole circumference. Fifths of the radius are found by laying five radii along the tangent from A, joining the fifth to O, and drawing parallels.

**Notes / Limitations:**
The scans of Hobson's book garble BC; 2/5 of the radius is the only value that gives his AE = 6.2831839. Hobson adds that the rectangle with sides AE and half the radius has very nearly the area of the circle, so the same construction squares the circle as a rectangle. Hobson gives no first name for Specht.
