**Verification Method:**
Rebuilt and recomputed by tools/verify.py, and checked against Finsler's formula AF = (8 − √62) a.

**File(s):**
DTC-99.9999-Finsler.ggb

**Description:**
"Man verlängere die Kante AB = a bis M und N, so daß AM = MN = 4a. Wird dann ME = MA und AE = AC gemacht, sowie NF = NE mit F auf AB, so ist AF sehr nahe ein Zehntel der gesuchten Würfelkante"

In the file M is found by doubling AB twice and N by the circle about M through A, which also carries E. AF is doubled three times and two more added, to F_10: AF_10 = 10 AF.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Over by 0.000050% in volume: 10 AF = 1.25992126 a against ∛2 a = 1.25992105 a. Selmer found the same value with continued fractions in 1957.
