**Description:**
Given the cube's edge AB: mark C and D along the line AB with AC = 2 AB and AD = 3 AB (circles about B and C). Draw circles about A through C and about C through A; their crossing F is the apex of an equilateral triangle on AC, so FB = √3 AB. Carry FB from D back towards A to mark G. Then AG = (3 − √3) AB = 1.2679 AB, against the true ∛2 AB = 1.2599 AB, so the volume is 2.0385 times the given cube's, against 2.
