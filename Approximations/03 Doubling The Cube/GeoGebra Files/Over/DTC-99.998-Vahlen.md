**Description:**
Vahlen: "Man konstruiere zunächst über AB = 1 das Quadrat ABCD und trage an AD das Stück DF = 5/9, an DC das Stück CE = 1 ab; dann ist EF² = 2² + (5/9)², BF² = 1² + (1 + 5/9)². Macht man dann noch FJ = 1, FG = FE und zieht GH ∥ BE und JK ∥ BH, so ist FK ≈ ∛2; denn es ist FK : FJ = FH : FB = FE² : FB²."

In the file AB is the edge and ABCD the square on it. DF = 5/9 is laid off below D: nine edges along DC produced (by doubling, and one more), five likewise, the line from the ninth to A and the parallel through the fifth, and the length carried below D. E is an edge beyond C. On FB, FJ is an edge and FG equals FE; the parallel to BE through G meets FE at H, and the parallel to BH through J meets FE at K. FK is the edge.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Over by 0.0005% of the edge (0.0016% by volume). 349/277 is a convergent of the continued fraction of ∛2; Vahlen's point is that it can be written as a ratio of two sums of squares, and so drawn with two right triangles and two parallels.
