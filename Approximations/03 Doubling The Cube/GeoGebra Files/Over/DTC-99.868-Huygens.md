**Description:**
Vahlen: "Wir erwähnen noch die von Huygens: Es sei AC = 2, AF = 60°, CD = 45°, dann ist AE = cosec 52,5° = 1,2605 ... ≈ ∛2; genauer müßte man EF = CD machen."

In the file the radius BC is the edge, and A the other end of the diameter. F is where the circle about A through B meets the circle (an arc of 60° from A); D is where the bisector of the right angle at B meets it (an arc of 45° from C). AD and CF meet at E. In the triangle AEC the angles at A and C are 22.5° and 30°, so AE = 2 sin 30° / sin 127.5° = cosec 52.5°.
