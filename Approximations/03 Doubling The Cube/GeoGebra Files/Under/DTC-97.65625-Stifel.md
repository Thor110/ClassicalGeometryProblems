**Description:**
Vahlen: "Eine mehr geometrische Konstruktion ist die von M. Stifel. In ihr ist zwar nur ∛2 = 5/4, aber sie läßt sich beliebig verbessern; sie beruht auf Platos Figur zur Einschaltung von zwei Mitteln. Um zwischen OA = 1 und OB = 2 die zwei Mittel OD = ∛2, OC = ∛4 einzuschalten, schlage man um M, wo OM = 3/8 (besser 0,37), mit MB den Kreis; der geht nahe durch C und D."

In Plato's figure D and C are where the right angles ADC and DCB fall, so the circle on DB as diameter passes through C. Stifel puts its centre at a fixed point. In the file OA is the edge, on the line y; x is perpendicular at O, and OB = 2 OA on it (E at one, B at two). M is three eighths of OA from O on OB (midpoints: M₁ at a half, M₂ at a quarter, M between them). The circle about M through B meets x beyond O at D, OD = 13/8 − 3/8 = 5/4, and y at C.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Short by 0.8% of the edge (2.3% by volume). Vahlen's improvement, OM = 0.37, gives OD = 1.26 (99.98% over by volume), but 0.37 is a decimal setting, not a construction step, so only Stifel's 3/8 is charted. The scan garbles the fractions; 3/8 and 5/4 are the only reading that fits both each other and the 0.37. The edge 5/4 is also what Gordon's Cube 2 Tesseract v1.0 gives (dtc-under-97.656), by a different construction; the label carries the whole value, 97.65625, to keep the two apart.
