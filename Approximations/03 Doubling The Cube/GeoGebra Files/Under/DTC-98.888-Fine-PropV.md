**Description:**
"Transibit enim indubitanter huius semicirculi peripheria per punctum e, ut ipsa te docebit experientia."

In the file B, C are b, c (bc the given edge) and A is a with ab = 2 bc. The golden section of gf is drawn with the usual right triangle (Euclid II.11), and the mean of fh and hg with a semicircle on gf: these steps are the archive's.

Finé gives the two means between the given lines ab and cd. The edge of the doubled cube is the mean next to the smaller line, so the file takes cd as the given edge and ab = 2 cd. Finé believed his constructions exact; the step he leaves to "experience" (that the last parallel falls on c) is where they fail.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Under by 1.111% in volume. Finé says the semicircle on ad "will undoubtedly pass through e"; it does not quite.
