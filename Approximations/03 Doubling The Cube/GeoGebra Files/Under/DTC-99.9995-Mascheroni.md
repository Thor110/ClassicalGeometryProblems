**Description:**
In Mascheroni's words: "Si on vouloit une plus grande approximation, après avoir fait la construction de la première solution, et avoir fait en outre AB = Ed = dc; Aa = BF = Db = db; aN = cM = MP; Fb = FQ; PQ sera le côté cherché."

d and c continue round the circle from E a radius at a time; b is where the circles of radius Aa about D and d meet (inside the circle, on AB); M is on the circle with cM = aN, P with MP = aN, and Q with FQ = Fb. Fifteen circles, no lines.

**Notes / Limitations:**
