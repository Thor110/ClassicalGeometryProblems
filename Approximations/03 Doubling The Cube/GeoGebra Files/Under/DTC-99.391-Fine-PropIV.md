**Problem:** Doubling The Cube

**Method Name:**
Finé's mean proportionals, Prop. IV (the square on the larger line)

**Author:**
Oronce Finé - "De rebus mathematicis, hactenus desideratis" (Paris 1556), book I, Prop. IV, fol. 15r-15v

**Accuracy (%):**
99.391
(precise readout: 99.391263788887016422452686329300)

**Oversized / Undersized:**
Undersized.

**Number of Circles:**
4

**Number of Lines:**
9

**Verification Method:**
Rebuilt and recomputed by tools/verify.py; the cube on the result is compared with twice the given cube.

**File(s):**
DTC-99.391-Fine-PropIV.ggb

**Description:**
"Ab ipso postmodum ac dimetiente, ipsi ab maiori secetur aequalis af: ipsi autem fc aequalis ag ... Per punctum deinde g ipsi ab parallela ducatur ghk, quae secet ad rectam in puncto h."

In the file C and D are the given c and d (cd the given edge), E is e with ce = 2 cd, and the square is completed to A and B.

Finé gives the two means between the given lines ab and cd. The edge of the doubled cube is the mean next to the smaller line, so the file takes cd as the given edge and ab = 2 cd. Finé believed his constructions exact; the step he leaves to "experience" (that the last parallel falls on c) is where they fail.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Under by 0.609% in volume. Finé's first mean, 3 − √2, is the square of the value √(3 − √2) that Sorgner later gave for ∛2 (1915), which is better.
