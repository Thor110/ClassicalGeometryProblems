**Description:**
In Mascheroni's words (art. 275): "Soit AB le côté du cube qu'on veut doubler; après avoir décrit du centre A avec le rayon AB la circonférence ..., et y avoir fait AB = BC = CD = DE; BD = Ba = Ea; Aa = BF; et FA = FN; aN sera, à très-peu de chose près, le côté du cube double."

Every step is a circle: C, D and E step round the circle a radius at a time; a is where the circles of radius BD about B and E meet; F is on the circle with BF = Aa (a quarter turn from B), and N with FN = FA (a twelfth of the circle from B). No line is drawn.

**Notes / Limitations:**
Short by 0.00064 of the edge (0.15% by volume). A construction of the compass alone, from Mascheroni's book showing that every straightedge and compass construction can be done without the straightedge.
