# Patch Notes

Changes to the Classical Geometry Problems site, newest first. These notes are mainly about the site itself:
its pages, charts, tools and checks. Each release also lists, in brief, how the archive of approximations
changed. The full record of every entry, with its sources, is in [pages/archive-data.js](pages/archive-data.js), each
entry's notes file, and [Wanted/README.md](Wanted/README.md).

The counting rules have their own version history, under "Changes" in [SUBMISSION_RULESET.md](SUBMISSION_RULESET.md).

**When you add a release:** put it at the top, under a heading with the date and a short title. Write
**Site** for changes to pages, charts and tools, and **Archive** for entries added, removed or re-credited.

## November 2026: Neusis by circles

### Site
- New page, [Neusis By Circles](pages/neusis.html): Archimedes' marked-ruler trisection with an interactive figure (set
  the angle, slide the ruler), why the ruler's final position cannot be drawn with straightedge and compass, why its path
  (a limaçon) can be drawn point by point, and how a circle through three points of that path gives the third within
  seconds of arc (3.6" at 90°). It explains Kopf's and Finsler's constructions as the same idea, and mentions iteration,
  paper folding and mechanical tools. An explainer only: nothing on it is charted.
- Linked from the home page, The Problems and Mechanical Solutions.

- Added Averdieck's trisection (1882), published by Schwering (1886), with the point D at a quarter of the angle as

## November 2026: Doubling the cube

### Archive
- Added six of Oronce Finé's constructions for two mean proportionals (De rebus mathematicis, 1556), which he believed exact:
  doubling the cube to 99.781% (over), 99.391%, 98.888%, 99.068% and 99.821% (under), and 99.850% (over). They are the
  earliest ruler-and-compass duplications on the chart after Stifel's.
- Johann Molther's "Problema Deliacum" (1619) gives Huygens' rectification, three diameters and a fifth of the quadrant's
  chord, 35 years before him: the credit for rtc-under-99.9945 moves to Molther, with Huygens and Busch under "Also found by".
- Václav Láska (1893) is added under "Also found by" on Vargiù's duplication: the same endless nest of mean proportionals.
- The Wanted page lists what the search found but could not chart: Finsler (1938; the paper's download needs a browser
  check), Sorgner and Michnik (1915), Lenzer (1930), Pappus' unnamed geometer, Casanova's rule (1790), Winthrop (1793),
  Di Gennaro (1802), Manoja (1836), Hullmann (1897), Dürer's and Molther's mechanical duplications, and Finé's gnomon.
- 216 entries, 73 authors.

  - `tools/verify.py` rebuilds every construction from its GeoGebra file and recomputes it in 50-digit arithmetic.
  - Each entry gets a precise 32-digit readout and an automated check list (`pages/ai-checks.js`).
  - Counting rules and the micro scale test: ruleset versions 1.0 to 1.2.
- `tools/geogebra_check.js` cross-checks constructions in GeoGebra itself.
- `tools/crossref.py` and `pages/crossref.js` find identical or near-identical entries.
- GitHub issue templates for submissions, verifications and disputes.
